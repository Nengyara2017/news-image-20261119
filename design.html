THE WORLD IN ONE DAY
THURSDAY, 19 NOVEMBER 2026
Diplomacy · Lead Story

Trump, Xi and Putin Hold the First-Ever Three-Way Summit

The three leaders met for 90 minutes in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the APEC forum, and issued a joint “peace declaration” on UN reform, oil supplies and nuclear risks — “a nuclear war cannot be won.” No breakthrough on Ukraine or Iran.

Justice

ICJ: Myanmar Committed Genocide of the Rohingya

By 9–6, the world court held a state guilty of genocide for the first time in 80 years. About 10,000 Rohingya were killed in 2017; it ordered reparations.

Ukraine

Kerch Bridge Hit a Third Time in Four Days

Neptune missiles hit the Crimean bridge’s rail section, halting trains; one track reopened by afternoon. Putin vowed a hard response.

Middle East

Iran War Spreads Across the Gulf

After the US struck a missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran fired drones at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain; a tanker was hit in Hormuz, one missing, eleven hurt.

Europe

Berlin: Allies Rally Around Ukraine

More than 40 partners of the Coalition of the Willing met in Berlin with Zelenskyy and US envoys Witkoff and Kushner. Kushner said Russia accepted Article 5-style guarantees; Trump called in.

Business

Anthropic’s $81.3 Billion IPO Is the Biggest Ever

Priced at $83 a share for a roughly $1.5 trillion valuation, passing SpaceX’s $75 billion record. Shares opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17 on the Nasdaq as ANT — up 17% on day one.
