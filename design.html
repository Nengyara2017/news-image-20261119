THURSDAY, 19 NOVEMBER 2026
Diplomacy · Lead Story

Trump, Xi and Putin Hold First-Ever Three-Way Summit

The three leaders met for 90 minutes behind closed doors in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the APEC forum, to discuss nuclear arms control, Ukraine and Iran. China and Russia issued a “peace declaration” that mentioned neither. No major agreement was announced.

Justice

ICJ: Myanmar Committed Genocide of Rohingya

By 9–6, the court held a state responsible for genocide for the first time in its 80-year history. About 10,000 Rohingya were killed in 2017; it ordered reparations.

Ukraine

Kerch Bridge Struck for a Third Time This Week

Ukraine hit the Crimean bridge’s rail section, halting train traffic. Russian strikes killed at least five people and knocked out power in five regions.

Middle East

Iran War Widens Across the Gulf

The US struck Iran’s Qeshm Island; Iran fired back at Kuwait and Bahrain, and a Houthi missile damaged Riyadh’s Public Investment Fund Tower.

Gaza

Israel Launches Ground Operation in Central Gaza

Israeli forces pushed beyond the Yellow Line near Nuseirat despite the ceasefire, in a new ground operation in central Gaza.

Business

Anthropic’s $81.3 Billion IPO Is the Biggest Ever

Anthropic raised a record $81.3 billion, priced at $83 a share for a roughly $1.5 trillion valuation, passing SpaceX’s $75 billion record. Shares began trading on the Nasdaq as ANT on Thursday.
